Notification surfaces inside `Dialog`

The [`Dialog`](https://ariakit.com/reference/dialog) component treats notification regions and announcers as persistent interaction surfaces. Notifications remain interactive and audible while a modal [`Dialog`](https://ariakit.com/reference/dialog) is open, without extra app configuration.
