Multi-value `Combobox` range selection

Multi-value [`Combobox`](https://ariakit.com/reference/combobox) and [`ComboboxSelect`](https://ariakit.com/reference/combobox-select) components now extend selected value arrays with Shift and the arrow keys. `ComboboxSelect` also applies range selection to Shift-click without extra item composition.
