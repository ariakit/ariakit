Fixed [`Hovercard`](https://ariakit.com/reference/hovercard) hiding while the pointer rests on another trigger element that took over its store, such as when moving the pointer from one [`Menubar`](https://ariakit.com/reference/menubar) item to the next with a shared [`Menu`](https://ariakit.com/reference/menu). This applies to all components built on [`Hovercard`](https://ariakit.com/reference/hovercard), including [`Menu`](https://ariakit.com/reference/menu).
